Pipeline Configuration

Currently: MAFFT + FastTree 2.2.0, terminal overhang trim, midpoint-rooted, 1000 bootstrap.

Choose the algorithm for multiple sequence alignment.

Remove poorly aligned regions to improve signal. The default drops columns that are more than 90% gaps, which clears alignment junk while leaving the variable ITS1/ITS2 regions intact.

trimAl -automated1 may remove the regions that identify your sequences

-automated1 is tuned for protein and phylogenomic alignments. On a 108-sequence fungal ITS test it kept 95% of the conserved 5.8S but discarded 42% of ITS1 and 45% of ITS2 — the variable regions that separate closely related fungi — cutting the alignment from 1316 to 449 columns, below the ~680 bp length of the sequences themselves.

The resulting tree was worse, not better: 24 well-supported nodes (SH-aLRT ≥ 80 and UFBoot ≥ 95) versus 32 with no trimming, and 79 resolved nodes versus 101, with mean support unchanged. The recommended gap-threshold option kept 318/320 of ITS1 and 199/201 of ITS2.

Fine for a locus where you know the ends are unreliable; not recommended for ITS.

Detect the chosen subregion with ITSx profiles and align only that region, so every sequence covers the same stretch of the locus. Sequences without enough of the target region are dropped and listed under the tree.

Sequences with less than this much of the region are dropped. Typical extracted lengths are ~200 bp for ITS1, ~160 bp for 5.8S, ~185 bp for ITS2, and ~570 bp for full ITS.

Tree Inference

Controls starting trees and early stopping logic.

E.g., GTR+G, JC69, HKY.

ModelFinder (-m MFP) tests the standard substitution models and infers the tree with whichever fits best by BIC. It usually adds only seconds to a run this size, and the model it chose is reported with the finished tree. Pick a specific model instead if you need to match a published analysis.

Select a sequence to root the tree. If selected, tree will NOT be midpoint rooted.

IQ-TREE requires >= 1000 for Ultrafast Bootstrap.

Runs the SH-aLRT branch test alongside Ultrafast Bootstrap. Nodes are then labelled SH-aLRT/UFBoot (both 0–100); a clade is conventionally called well supported at SH-aLRT ≥ 80 and UFBoot ≥ 95. Set to 0 to report UFBoot only.

Relative burn-in is discarded from MrBayes convergence diagnostics and from the sump/sumt summaries. The default is 25%.
