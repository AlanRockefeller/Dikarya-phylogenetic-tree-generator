Find the correct iNaturalist observation

Enter the observation number you have, plus any other information you know. You can leave any of the clues blank. The search works through the common transcription errors on its own and stops as soon as everything you told it lines up.

The search runs in your browser and talks directly to iNaturalist. Dikarya does not store your query.

          How the search widens

          1. First — the number exactly as you typed it, in case it is right and a clue is the thing that is wrong.
          2. Then — one substituted digit, two adjacent digits swapped, and a missing or extra digit at any position.
          3. Then — two substituted digits, and finally three, asking first when a step is a large one.

          It finishes a step before stopping, so you see every equally good candidate rather than just the first. Observations that match only some of your clues are kept and shown too.

          iNaturalist numbers observations in the order they were uploaded, so the numbers either side of a mistyped one often belong to the same person. If the answer is not in the results, Keep looking carries on from where it stopped.
