iNaturalist Observation Finder

Recover an observation when one or more digits were transcribed incorrectly. Search by the expected genus, family, taxon ID, observer, or iNaturalist project.

The search runs in your browser and talks directly to iNaturalist. Dikarya does not store your query.

1. What should match?

    Five digits or fewer may be a Mushroom Observer number.

    Which taxon did you mean?

    Choosing one searches by its iNaturalist taxon ID, which also matches everything below it.

    • Changed digits at every position
    • A missing digit at any position for short IDs
    • One or two extra digits in long IDs
    • Two adjacent digits typed the wrong way round
    • The original ID, in case it already matches
