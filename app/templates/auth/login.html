Why create an account?

  • Exclusive edit access. Only you can prune, reroot, rename tips, or recompute your trees. Without an account, anyone who has the job link can modify the tree.
  • My Jobs dashboard. See all of your past analyses in one place, instead of having to bookmark each job URL.
  • Persistent history. Your jobs stay tied to your account so you can come back to them later.

Prefer not to sign up? You can use Dikarya anonymously without an account — build trees, share links, and download results just the same. The only tradeoff is that anyone with a job link can also edit that tree.
