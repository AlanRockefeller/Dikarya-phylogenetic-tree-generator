Author Fees

There are none. Dikarya does not charge article processing charges (APCs), submission fees, or publication fees of any kind, and readers are never charged either. No author is turned away for inability to pay, because there is nothing to pay.

That is what the support on this page is for: donations, sponsorship, and volunteered time are what keep publication free for authors and readers alike.
