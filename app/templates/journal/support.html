Author Fees

To support publication costs, we charge a modest article processing charge (APC) upon acceptance:

  • Research Articles: $400 USD
  • Short Communications: $200 USD
  • Reviews: By invitation (no fee)

Fee Waivers

We offer full or partial fee waivers based on financial need. Authors from low-income countries or without institutional funding are encouraged to request waivers during submission. No author will be turned away for inability to pay.
